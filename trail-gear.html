Find your way

A phone is great until the battery or signal quits. These are my backups.

Compass

Suunto A-10 baseplate compass

A simple, reliable compass made for reading paper maps.

Mapmaker tip: Set it on a printed trail map and turn both until north lines up. That one move makes a map make sense.

Garmin eTrex SE handheld GPS

A budget GPS that runs on two AA batteries for days.

Hands-free light that charges by USB. A best seller in camping gear.

Mapmaker tip: Use red-light mode to read a map at night without ruining your night vision.
