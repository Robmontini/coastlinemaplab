How it works

Six questions every land buyer should ask the soil.

Each step shows one layer on the map, so you only learn one thing at a time.

  1. Is the soil sour?Soil pH, and when lime can fix it.
  2. Do roots sit in water?Drainage classes and wet spots.
  3. Will it last through summer?How much water the soil holds.
  4. Is it too steep or too low?Slope, flood zones, and wetlands.
  5. How does it score overall?A weighted map you can change yourself.
  6. How many good acres?Clip to the parcel and add it up.
