About

A North Coast mapmaker who likes explaining things simply.

I’m Rob Montini, a GIS and cartography student based in Crescent City, California. I started Coastline Map Labs to turn what I’m learning into maps that help people make real decisions, like whether a piece of land will support a home, a garden, or a well-placed septic system.

My work spans QGIS cartography, soil and land analysis, 3D terrain maps, and StoryMaps. I’m also learning Python to automate GIS work. Through GIS Made Simple, I make short, calm, beginner-friendly lessons for people who want to make their own maps.

If you need a map, a report, or a patient teacher, I’d love to hear from you.
